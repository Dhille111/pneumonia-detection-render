Spotting Anomalies on Radiographs

Typical markers of pneumonia infections inside lung boundaries.

Lung Consolidation

Healthy lungs show as dark/black voids (since air does not obstruct X-rays). In pneumonia, fluid, pus, or cellular debris fills alveolar cavities, showing as fuzzy white patches (consolidation).

Interstitial Infiltrates

Often diffuse in viral pneumonia, presenting as hazy 'ground-glass' patterns or web-like structures spread across lung fields, usually bilateral.

Costophrenic Angles

The bottom sharp corners of lung voids. If blunt or cloudy, it suggests fluid buildup (pleural effusion), a common secondary complication of pneumonia.

Clinical Note: AI screening provides a binary probability estimate. High-density structures like bones, implants, breast tissue, or bad posture lines can cause false positives and must be visually excluded.
